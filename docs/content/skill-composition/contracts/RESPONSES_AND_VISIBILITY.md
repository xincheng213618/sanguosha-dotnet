# Responses and visibility

Required when changing these mechanisms or their consumers. See the
[contract index](README.md) for related topics.

## Cursor suspension

- Issued native cursor suspensions use `INativeCursorSuspensions` and immutable declarations on the actual original frame. Keep the live issuer and attack/continuation, dying and Nullification scopes distinct; capture their real paid/issued intervals once and release only that issuer's scope. Visibility reads do not requalify skills or scan character proof histories, and suspension does not change the original attack state or authorize new effects. Public Hand reveal retains its own window protocol within the shared visibility answer; continuation qualification reads only issued declarations and its actual issuer window, never aggregate visibility.

## Response requirements and applied relations

- Issued target response requirements use the actual incoming frame's `ResponseRules` and the shared Lifecycle query; direct program uses retain their original instruction scope. Distinguish the card actor, current opponent, logical responder and material provider. Minimum requirements combine by max and prohibitions by OR. Never find a response rule by searching for a character-marked ancestor; keep current-attack cancellation, counter-Nullification and dying rescue boundaries distinct.

- Whole-use response prohibitions share that same rule data with an explicit audience, including non-target responders and both Duel directions. Source attribution supports explicit withdrawal of that source's applied policy; source loss or death does not itself withdraw issued rules. Native compound instructions return to the original program cursor so ordinary JSON steps can consume their declared outputs.

- The native Duel cursor records each physical response material entering the discard pile in its own response completion. The record keeps the responding seat and actual entry sequence before the cursor advances. A claim checks the original response entry and can only move entries that are still current.

- A native Slash keeps the actual discard-pile entries of its accepted physical Dodge responses when a completed-use offer needs them. The original Slash owns the response and its cleanup entries, including a Dodge supplied by another player. A gift of that response requires all its materials to remain in their original entries. The response still returns to the native Slash cursor when its materials are unavailable.

- Native Bagua starts at `CoreNativeJudgmentRuntime.ResolveBaguaJudgment` for Slash, Arrow Barrage and Qizheng. This owner applies an immediate outcome once. A suspended judgment returns through its original `JudgmentFrame`. A registered `ReturnBaguaResponse` handler may accept the outcome on its original use, as Qizheng does. Ordinary Slash and Arrow Barrage keep their native response continuation. An already-issued equipment invocation and a faction-defense provider keep their own return paths.

- The shared replacement owner collects program retrial candidates and issues and validates the private blind deck-top choice. A blind candidate qualifies when either the draw pile or the discard pile has material. The offer contains no card ID. Acceptance may recycle the discard pile and then freezes the actual top card as payment on the original judgment. Declining continues that judgment with its original card. Ordinary candidate collection defers a required color difference to the live offer and payment checks.

- Gouxiang checks the exact effective armor and matching equipment skill instance when the original Dodge response completes. The original attack supplies armor bypass and the compared card appearance. The qualified effect acquires one current hand entity from the original Slash actor through native movement.

- A response exchange reads the accepted response's public entries on its native parent. A direct claim checks those entries before offering and acquiring the materials. When the exchange gives the original card first, it freezes the response entries before delivery and rechecks them after the delivery's children return. Loss of a response entry leaves the earlier delivery and its restriction effective.

- When a counterspell replies to another counterspell, its native window retains only the immediately answered payment receipt alongside its own. An exchange giving that earlier play verifies its action, chain position and current physical entries before delivery.

- A completed-response storage offer reads the actual discard entry from the response window's `NativeCosts` after the payment children settle. Its candidate and execution check that same current entry, since the same physical card can leave and reenter the discard pile before the original window resumes.

- Applied response relations use `IAppliedResponseRules` and the shared lifetime store; native use and current counterspell nodes capture issued rules on their original frames. Keep exact displayed names distinct from normalized basic names, frozen source-holder color distinct from unknown color, and declared boundary expiry distinct from turn-number expiry. Query the real current counterspell action/depth; never inherit the root trick after depth zero, use character-origin whitelists, or requalify issued rules after source loss. Real delivery freezes the relation before paid notifications; card-actor, responder and material-provider identities remain separate.

- `INativeCounterspellResponseContinuations` prepares, binds and completes the real counterspell on the original window. Physical, tiered zero-entity, combined-material and 绝言 producers advance the real depth and bind the accepted action before its Accepted notification. Read `ResponseRuleNode.Action` to decide the single actual flip, including with no observers. Preserve public Responded and the typed producer return once. Continuous response restrictions, attack-specific response negation and special counterspell payments still have separate composition work.

- A delayed trick's judgment-phase counterspell checks the original `DelayedEffectContinuation` and reads the currently held judgment card and its effective kind. Payment observation and delayed completion share that read. A separate original-use Lightning or a claimed delayed effect uses its own native receipt.

## Card-action invalidation

- Accepted native card-action ineffectiveness uses `INativeCardActionInvalidations` on the original owning frame. Whole-use state, an individual Dodge action and an exact counterspell action/depth are distinct facts; a cancelled response never cancels its parent use. Keep early and before-target-effects simple-card cleanup boundaries, declared effect sets, priority and physical move reasons separate; a whole-use flag alone never authorizes skipping the original paid tail. Dodge cancellation covers actual Slash defense and Arrow Barrage group responses, including the shared OL/Wushuang Zengou recipe; its cost, HP and material acquisition retain their real owners. Native producer returns stay in their existing native owner, without adding character dependencies to the shared invalidation executor. Remaining-card-damage suppression requires its explicit issued permission and the actual native damage-owner check. Source qualification, real payments, status consumption and equipment-window admission stay at their actual producers; their public events are business facts, not repeated validity proofs. Do not requalify an issued cancellation after source loss or rebuild it from character events.

## Public reveal windows and history

- Public Hand reveal producers use `IPublicHandRevealWindows` for issued candidates, suspension and returns. Keep source qualification outside that shared owner, consume issued candidates by the original frame cursor, and return to each producer's actual owner. Do not prove window progress by scanning character events; declare source-loss and owner-death invocation permissions separately.

- Public show sources use the existing canonical reveal events and `IPublicCardRevealQueries`; hand residency uses `ICurrentHandEntryQueries` over the physical movement journal. Keep reveal occurrences, remembered visibility during one hand entry and skill-specific quotas distinct. Character rules retain their actual sampling times and do not rebuild source switches or borrow another character's frame-proof helpers.

- Public show history uses `IPublicCardRevealHistoryQueries` over shared native samples. Capture only source facts missing from the original reveal event; rules retain their actual residency, phase, turn and material-disclosure semantics instead of rebuilding character event ledgers.
